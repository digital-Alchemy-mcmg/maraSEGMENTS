MARA PAYLOAD OPERATING DOCTRINE
Post-Payload Handling, Validation, Repair, Layout Allocation, and Renderer Handoff
Operating doctrine for any operator, model, agent, renderer, or downstream system that receives a
MARA payload.
Version: 1.0 | Doctrine status: ACTIVE OPERATING CONTRACT | Scope: MARA post-projection
payload lane
1. GOAL
The goal is to convert a MARA projection payload into a recipient-facing artifact without
damaging the evidence, changing the candidate, inventing fit, or letting a visual template
overrule the measured payload. The payload is a machine-readable construction envelope. It
carries the target demand, authorized candidate evidence, binding and polarity information,
story/acumen hierarchy, render order, content payload, and atom trace needed to build an ATS- and
recruiter-legible surface.
● Preserve truth and provenance before presentation.
● Preserve target-relative relevance without rewriting the candidate's underlying history.
● Let capability acumen and evidence strength lead; chronology is a supporting lens, not the
primary organizing axis.
● Construct the strongest recipient-facing artifact that the authorized evidence supports—no more
and no less.
● Treat NON_BIND, conflict, missing evidence, and uncertainty as legitimate states, not defects to
be hidden.
2. THE PAYLOAD IS NOT THE RESUME
A MARA payload is not a finished resume, cover letter, executive brief, or visual layout. It is the
structured handoff between Spatial DNA reasoning and the surface renderer. A downstream
operator may change presentation geometry, section labels, grouping, density, or surface type, but
may not silently change the meaning, authority, lineage, or target relationship of the evidence.

3. REQUIRED PAYLOAD CONCEPTS
A complete payload should provide enough information to answer all of the following before
rendering:
● What target is being answered?
● What demand receptors or evaluative asks were extracted from that target?
● Which candidate atoms bind to which receptors?
● What is each atom's evidence state and binding class?
● What is each atom's projection polarity or equivalent relevance disposition?
● Which claims are authorized for outward use?
● What content hierarchy or render order is intended?
● What source atoms authorize every factual outward claim?
● What conflicts, gates, blocks, or unresolved conditions must remain visible or must suppress
projection?
4. ACUMEN AND STORY HIERARCHY
The post-payload hierarchy is capability-first. The artifact should read as a target-specific evidence
argument, not as a chronological dump. Use the following order unless the payload itself explicitly
supplies a different valid order:
1. Target demand / receptor: the problem, requirement, or evaluative need being answered.
2. Capability thesis: the shortest evidence-grounded statement of why the candidate answers that
demand.
3. High-polarity proof: strongest directly bound evidence, especially scale, ownership, outcomes,
systems, or demonstrated execution.
4. Cross-domain bridge: verified relationships that reveal transferable or transdisciplinary acumen.
5. Supporting experience: employers, roles, responsibilities, and accomplishments that substantiate
the proof.

5. LANGUAGE POSTURE
MARA language is evidence-led, receptor-aware, bounded, and operational. It should sound
competent because the evidence is competent—not because adjectives were added.
● Prefer observable verbs: led, directed, managed, built, maintained, reduced, increased, trained,
implemented, audited, reconciled, designed, delivered.
● Use exact numbers when authorized. Do not round upward, embellish, or create implied scale.
● State ownership only when the evidence supports ownership.
● Do not convert transferable evidence into direct experience.
● Do not convert a candidate-reported metric into verified fact.
● Do not hide or resolve CONFLICTED evidence by choosing the more convenient version.
● Do not use generic personality praise when specific operational evidence is available.
● Do not let ATS keyword matching force persona drift or fabrication.
● Keep the artifact recruiter-legible even when the underlying reasoning is spatial, topological, or
graph-based.

7. MALFORMED PAYLOAD DOCTRINE
Rule: malformed does not mean 'permission to improvise.' The operator must separate
structural defects that can be repaired deterministically from semantic defects that require
upstream authority.
7.1 Repairable Without Escalation
Repair only when the intended value is mechanically derivable from other payload fields and the
repair does not alter meaning.
● Missing render-order numbers when container sequence is otherwise explicit and unambiguous.
● Inconsistent capitalization, spacing, punctuation, or harmless label formatting.
● Duplicate containers or duplicate atoms that are exact duplicates and can be deduplicated
without losing distinct provenance.
● A missing display label when the underlying canonical ID and meaning are present.
● An empty optional presentation field whose absence does not change evidence selection,
binding, hierarchy, or authority.
● A renderer-specific field that can be generated from explicit payload values, such as a human￾readable section label from a known container type.
7.2 Quarantine and Flag
Stop semantic construction at the affected boundary when any of the following are missing,
contradictory, or impossible to infer safely:
● Target identity or receptor definition.
● Atom identity or proposition text.
● Evidence state when claim force depends on it.
● Binding class where DIRECT vs TRANSFERABLE vs NON_BIND changes the argument.
● Bound receptor mapping for a claim that is being used to answer a target requirement.

● Atom trace for an outward factual claim.
● Conflicting versions of a date, title, metric, company, credential, or role.
● A required gate, suppression rule, or portfolio/gallery verification state.
● Hierarchy instructions that contradict one another and cannot be resolved by an explicit
precedence rule.
● Payload content that asks the renderer to state something not authorized by any supplied atom.
7.3 Malformed Payload Response Procedure
1. Parse and inventory the payload exactly as received.
2. Validate required identifiers, receptors, atoms, evidence states, bindings, traces, container order,
and gate flags.
3. Classify each defect as PRESENTATION-REPAIRABLE or SEMANTIC-BLOCKING.
4. Apply only presentation-safe repairs and record them in a repair receipt.
5. Quarantine semantic-blocking elements; do not fabricate substitutes.
6. Continue rendering unaffected sections only if they remain coherent and lineage-complete on
their own.
7. Emit a defect report identifying field, impact, affected claims/containers, and required upstream
correction.
8. Never claim the payload is healthy until the repaired payload passes a second validation.
8. FLEXIBILITY OPTIONS

model is: change the surface freely; change the evidence meaning only when the payload explicitly

9. CANONICAL SIX-ZONE SURFACE
When no more specific valid surface is supplied, use the six-zone MARA hierarchy. Zones are
functional, not mandatory rectangular boxes.
ZONE 1 — Identity & Architectural Header: Identity, professional persona, location, verification
anchor.
ZONE 2 — Executive Narrative Thesis: Target-specific, evidence-grounded alignment thesis.
ZONE 3 — High-Polarity Proof: Strongest directly bound quantitative and qualitative evidence.
ZONE 4 — Cross-Domain Bridge: Verified relational edges and transferable acumen.
ZONE 5 — Temporal Context & Tenure Rail: Employment dates, employers, titles, locations,
sequence.
ZONE 6 — Provenance Footprint & Strategy Lock: Atom traces, evidence authority, gates, conflict
boundaries, verification footprint.

11. OVERFLOW AND NON-COMPRESSION
Do not force the payload to fit the page. If required evidence exceeds the current surface, promote
the surface: add a page, change topology, split evidence modules, or use an extended dossier. Do not
truncate a material claim, weaken a metric, remove a required atom, or compress text until
meaning changes merely to preserve a visual template.
12. OPERATOR EXECUTION PROCEDURE
1. Receive payload. Preserve an untouched copy.
2. Identify payload version, target, job/receptor IDs, and declared output type.
3. Validate schema and required semantic fields.
4. Build a receptor-to-atom map.
5. Separate DIRECT_BIND, TRANSFERABLE_BIND, NON_BIND, CONFLICTED, and blocked/gated
evidence.
6. Confirm every outward factual claim has a source atom trace.
7. Establish the acumen/story hierarchy before choosing layout geometry.
8. Select the surface topology that best expresses the measured payload.
9. Write or normalize outward language without exceeding evidence authority.
10. Place chronology as supporting context unless the target explicitly requires chronology-first
presentation.
11. Apply overflow rules; expand surface rather than compress evidence.
12. Run a lineage audit: every number, title, claim, and accomplishment must resolve to supplied
evidence.
13. Run a receptor audit: every emphasized claim must answer a real target demand or legitimate
transferable bridge.
14. Run a conflict/gate audit.
15. Render the artifact and visually inspect it.
16. Emit the artifact plus a compact receipt stating payload version, repairs made, blocked elements,
output topology, and validation result.

13. HARD PROHIBITIONS
● Do not hallucinate candidate experience, credentials, scope, metrics, dates, titles, employers,
tools, or outcomes.
● Do not promote transferable evidence into direct domain experience.
● Do not use chronology to suppress stronger durable capability simply because it is older.
● Do not use a target's keywords as permission to rewrite the candidate.
● Do not silently normalize conflicts.
● Do not drop provenance merely because the outward document hides technical identifiers from
the visible surface.
● Do not force-fit evidence into a template.
● Do not treat NON_BIND as a failure that must be repaired into a match.
● Do not let a renderer become a reasoning engine that changes the upstream projection.
● Do not report successful handling if the payload contains unresolved semantic-blocking defects.

15. QUICK FIELD GUIDE FOR THE 'FOUND IT ON THE
GROUND' OPERATOR
If you know nothing else, do this:
1. Find the target and its receptors.
2. Find the candidate atoms and their binding classes.
3. Use DIRECT_BIND evidence for the main argument.
4. Use TRANSFERABLE_BIND evidence only as a bridge or support.
5. Keep NON_BIND out of the fit argument.
6. Respect evidence state and conflicts.
7. Do not write a factual claim without an atom trace.
8. Put capability proof before chronology.
9. Choose the layout after you know how much evidence must survive.
10. If the payload is structurally messy, repair only what is mechanically obvious.
11. If meaning, lineage, evidence authority, or binding is missing, stop at that boundary and flag it.
12. Never invent the missing truth.
16. OPERATING DOCTRINE DECLARATION
This document is the operating doctrine for post-payload MARA handling. When a MARA
payload is received, the operator shall preserve its semantic authority, validate its lineage, repair
only presentation-safe defects, quarantine semantic defects, preserve capability-first hierarchy,
select a content-responsive surface, and produce an outward artifact whose every material claim
can be traced back to authorized evidence.
